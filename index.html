MyFinote Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MyFinote ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using MyFinote, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Financial Data: Budget names, transaction records, and expense categories are stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Backup & Restore feature.

2. Data Storage & Security

All financial data is stored locally using encrypted storage. We do not upload this data to any server. We use Firebase Authentication for secure identity verification only.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google LLC) for optional sign-in and anonymized crash reporting. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
